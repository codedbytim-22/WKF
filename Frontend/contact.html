Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a donation?

You can donate online via our secure donation form using credit card, M-PESA, or PayPal. You can also send a bank transfer – contact us for details.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, WKF is a registered NGO in Kenya. Donations are tax-deductible, and we issue official receipts upon request.

Can I volunteer with WKF?

Absolutely! We welcome volunteers for various roles. Please fill out the contact form with "Volunteer" in the subject, and we'll get back to you.

How can I partner with WKF?

We partner with corporations, foundations, and community organizations. Reach out to our partnerships team at partnerships@wkf.org.
