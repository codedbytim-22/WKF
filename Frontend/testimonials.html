Stories of Impact

Hear from the children, families, donors, and partners whose lives have been touched by your support.

"Before WKF, I had lost hope of finishing school. They gave me a scholarship and a mentor who believed in me. Today, I am in my second year of nursing school. I want to give back to my community just like they did for me."

Mercy Akinyi

Scholarship Beneficiary

"I've been donating to WKF monthly for three years. What I love most is the transparency – I receive updates on exactly how my money is used and the progress of programs. It's rewarding to see real change."

Michael Omondi

Monthly Donor

"The peace dialogues facilitated by WKF brought together our community which was deeply divided. For the first time in years, we sat down, listened to each other, and found common ground. Our children now play together without fear."

Fatuma Hassan

Community Leader, Garissa

"Volunteering as a mentor with WKF has been one of the most fulfilling experiences. Seeing the transformation in the youth – their confidence, their dreams – reminds me why I became a teacher."

James Kariuki

Volunteer Mentor

"Our school partnership with WKF brought a new library and computer lab. Attendance has improved, and our students' performance has never been better. We are grateful for this partnership."

Mary Wanjiru

Principal, Mwiki Primary

"My daughter was shy and lacked confidence. Through WKF's empowerment program, she discovered her leadership skills and even led a community cleanup. I can't thank them enough."

David Otieno

Parent
