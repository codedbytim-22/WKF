Our Programs & Initiatives

We run three core programs that address the root causes of poverty and conflict, empowering children and communities to build a better future.

Education for All

Goal: $50,000

Raised: $37,500 75%

We believe education is the most powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty. Our Education for All program provides:

  • Full and partial scholarships for primary and secondary school
  • Learning materials including books, uniforms, and supplies
  • School infrastructure improvements (classrooms, libraries, water tanks)
  • After-school tutoring and mentorship

Impact: 2,500+ children supported, 25 partner schools, 90% pass rate among scholarship recipients.

Child Empowerment

Goal: $30,000

Raised: $18,000 60%

We equip children and youth with life skills, leadership abilities, and confidence to become change-makers in their communities. Our empowerment initiatives include:

  • Mentorship program connecting youth with professional role models
  • Life skills training: financial literacy, communication, critical thinking
  • Leadership camps and youth forums
  • Girl-child specific programs addressing early marriage and teen pregnancy
  • Boy-child initiatives focusing on positive masculinity

Impact: 1,800+ youth trained, 30 active mentors, 15 youth-led community projects.

Donate to Empowerment

Peace Advocacy

Goal: $40,000

Raised: $18,000 45%

We promote peaceful coexistence and conflict resolution in communities affected by ethnic tensions and resource-based conflicts. Our peace program includes:

  • Community peace dialogues bringing together diverse groups
  • Peace education in schools teaching tolerance and conflict resolution
  • Training of peace ambassadors (youth and women)
  • Inter-ethnic sports and cultural events
  • Advocacy campaigns for policy change

Impact: 12 communities engaged, 200+ peace ambassadors trained, 30% reduction in reported conflicts in target areas.
