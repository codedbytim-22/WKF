About Wanjiku Kimiti Foundation

Empowering communities through education, child welfare, and sustainable peace initiatives.

Our Mission

To empower children and youth through access to quality education, life skills, and peacebuilding, creating a future where every child can thrive in a safe and supportive environment.

Our Vision

A Kenya where all children, regardless of background, have equal opportunities to learn, lead, and live in peace.

Our Story

Wanjiku Kimiti Foundation was founded in 2015 by Wanjiku Kimiti, a dedicated educator and peace advocate who witnessed firsthand the barriers children face in accessing education and living in conflict-affected communities.

What started as a small scholarship fund for 10 girls in Kiambu County has grown into a national organization reaching over 5,000 children annually across 12 counties. Our holistic approach combines educational support, mentorship, and community peace dialogues.

Today, we partner with 25 schools, 40 community groups, and hundreds of volunteers to create lasting change. Our work is driven by the belief that every child has potential, and peace is the foundation for development.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We act with empathy and kindness, putting the well-being of children first in all we do.

Integrity

We are transparent, accountable, and ethical in our operations and relationships.

Collaboration

We work together with communities, partners, and donors to achieve sustainable impact.

Innovation

We embrace creative solutions and adapt to changing needs to maximize our impact.
